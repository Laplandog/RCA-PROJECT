05
The Object
A closed, egg-like form that sits in the space it listens to — a diffuse glowing body with probes that reach into soil, water or air.
A

Diffuser shell

A closed, egg-shaped translucent enclosure that turns the LED core into a soft, whole-body glow — the object’s “skin.”

B

Light core

Addressable WS2812 LEDs driven from the Pi — the single expressive organ carrying hue, breath and flow.

C

Cognitive core

Raspberry Pi 4B runs the liquid neural network, the journal, and the local PWA server over Wi-Fi.

D

Sensory board

Arduino Uno with the seven sensors, linked to the Pi by a single internal USB-C data line.

E

Power

A 21700 battery tower, 5V / 4A, pogo-pin fed — fully self-contained, no tethered supply.

F

Probes

Waterproof temperature, pH and EC probes reach out of the shell into the medium being sensed.
