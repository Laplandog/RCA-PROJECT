ANIMA / A LIVING PRESENCE
Product Design

A sensing body
for an environment
you cannot feel.

A self-contained ambient object that gives an ecosystem a body. Environment flows in through sensory organs; a liquid neural network translates it into a felt state; the object breathes it back as light. One body — a genome re-weights the senses so it can inhabit soil, water, or air.
