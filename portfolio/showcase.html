ANIMA / A LIVING PRESENCE
An ambient life that feels the environment it lives in — and tells you how it feels.

An emotional translation device for the ecological environment.

ANIMA gives an environment a body and an inner life. Streams of environmental sensor data — temperature, soil moisture, air quality, light, sound, pH, nutrients — are read on a microcontroller, weighted through a genome that defines what this life cares about, and translated by a liquid neural network (CfC) into a five-dimensional emotional state. That state becomes colour, breathing rhythm, and a first-person journal of lived experience. A life is born by choosing an ecological niche, grows continuously, can be cryo-frozen and later revived, reborn, or let go.
