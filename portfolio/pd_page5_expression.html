From Feeling to Light
The five-dimensional emotion becomes non-verbal physical expression. It conveys what the environment is going through — never what the device “wants.”
The light core breathes at ~4.5 s
calm · secure · quietly growing
Valencedistress ↔ pleasure
LED hue

Positive → green / cyan; negative → orange / red; neutral → warm white.

Arousalcalm ↔ aroused
Breathing speed

Low arousal → slow, gentle brightening and dimming; high → quick, urgent flicker.

Safetyalert ↔ secure
Stability

Secure → steady and even; unsafe → a faint tremor or wavering brightness.

Connectionisolated ↔ answered
Flowing ring

When it senses a response — a person nearing, sound shifting — a ring of light flows around the core.

Seekingcontent ↔ needful
Pulse + optional tone

Unrelieved stress raises the pulse rate; sound is reserved for extremes — never speech.
