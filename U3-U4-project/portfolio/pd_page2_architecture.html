System Architecture
The signal chain that turns invisible environmental stress into a feeling you can read at a glance.
Source

Environment

A soil bed, a body of water, or the air in a room — an ecosystem whose state is invisible to us.

Sensory organs

Arduino Uno

Seven analog / digital sensors sampled and calibrated into physical units on the board.

Nerve

USB · JSON

A line of JSON every ~2 s over USB serial at 115200 baud — the nervous signal to the brain.

Cognitive core

Raspberry Pi 4B

A liquid neural network (CfC + AutoNCP) reads the stream against the environment’s own rhythm.

Inner state

5-D emotion

Valence · arousal · safety · connection · seeking — a felt state, not a threshold alarm.

Expression

Light & breath

Colour, breathing rhythm and flow on an addressable LED core — non-verbal, at a glance.
