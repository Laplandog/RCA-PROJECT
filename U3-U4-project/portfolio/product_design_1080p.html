Sensory Organs
Seven universal channels — one shared body of senses. Every genome listens to all of them; it only cares about them differently.
🌡️
CH · T

Temperature

DS18B20 · waterproof probe

The core of metabolism — the internal temperature of the soil or water the life is rooted in.

💧
CH · M

Moisture

Capacitive soil probe

Thirst — substrate water content. Zeroed when submerged or in air, where it does not apply.

🫧
CH · G

Air & VOC

BME680 · T/RH/pressure/VOC

Breath & staleness — a sudden VOC rise hints at rot, poor ventilation, or an unusual source.

☀️
CH · L

Light

BH1750 · lux (digital)

Day & night rhythm — dark at night is normal; bright when it should be dark is a disturbed cycle.

🔊
CH · S

Sound

INMP441 mic · peak-to-peak

Disturbance — mechanical agitation, water flow, or sustained noise as an independent stressor.

⚗️
CH · pH

Acidity

PH-4502C · glass electrode

Chemical comfort — the acidity of the medium; slow drift out of range affects nutrient uptake.

⚡
CH · EC

Nutrients

TDS module · conductivity

Nourishment — ion concentration: too low reads as starvation, too high as salt stress.

🧬
Principle

Complete senses

A channel weighted to zero is still recorded — the body always senses fully; the genome only decides what it feels.

The Genome — one body, many natures
A genome is not different hardware — it is a sensory weighting table: a weight (0–1), a comfort range and a sensitivity curve per channel. Switching it lets the same object inhabit a different niche.

The Object
A closed, egg-like form that sits in the space it listens to — a diffuse glowing body with probes that reach into soil, water or air.
A

Diffuser shell

A closed, egg-shaped translucent enclosure that turns the LED core into a soft, whole-body glow — the object’s “skin.”

B

Light core

Addressable WS2812 LEDs driven from the Pi — the single expressive organ carrying hue, breath and flow.

C

Cognitive core

Raspberry Pi 4B runs the liquid neural network, the journal, and the local PWA server over Wi-Fi.

D

Sensory board

Arduino Uno with the seven sensors, linked to the Pi by a single internal USB-C data line.

E

Power

A 21700 battery tower, 5V / 4A, pogo-pin fed — fully self-contained, no tethered supply.

F

Probes

Waterproof temperature, pH and EC probes reach out of the shell into the medium being sensed.

Why a Liquid Neural Network
Three architectural principles make the object smarter than a threshold alarm — and true to its purpose.
01

Rate of change over absolute value

The CfC network flags a departure from the environment’s own recent rhythm. The same temperature drop is danger in spring and normal at night — context, not a hard-coded number.

02

Multi-channel consistency

A single drifting sensor is usually noise. An event is declared only when two independent channels trend the same way — sensor fusion that suppresses false alarms.

03

Zero weight ≠ blind

Muted channels are still logged, leaving room for the object to one day suggest “this looks more like water — switch genome?” The senses stay whole; only attention shifts.
