Sensory Organs
Seven universal channels — one shared body of senses. Every genome listens to all of them; it only cares about them differently.
🌡️
CH · T

Temperature

DS18B20 · waterproof probe

The core of metabolism — the internal temperature of the soil or water the life is rooted in.

💧
CH · M

Moisture

Capacitive soil probe

Thirst — substrate water content. Zeroed when submerged or in air, where it does not apply.

🫧
CH · G

Air & VOC

BME680 · T/RH/pressure/VOC

Breath & staleness — a sudden VOC rise hints at rot, poor ventilation, or an unusual source.

☀️
CH · L

Light

BH1750 · lux (digital)

Day & night rhythm — dark at night is normal; bright when it should be dark is a disturbed cycle.

🔊
CH · S

Sound

INMP441 mic · peak-to-peak

Disturbance — mechanical agitation, water flow, or sustained noise as an independent stressor.

⚗️
CH · pH

Acidity

PH-4502C · glass electrode

Chemical comfort — the acidity of the medium; slow drift out of range affects nutrient uptake.

⚡
CH · EC

Nutrients

TDS module · conductivity

Nourishment — ion concentration: too low reads as starvation, too high as salt stress.

🧬
Principle

Complete senses

A channel weighted to zero is still recorded — the body always senses fully; the genome only decides what it feels.
