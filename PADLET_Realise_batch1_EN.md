## 2 · RE-I0

**Title:** What This Phase Is Not

**Body:**

Three refusals, stated up front so nothing later can quietly claim them.

**Not a second experiment.**  
Investigate already ran the twin-track (nurtured / wild). Realise does not re-ask “will behaviour emerge?” as if that work had not happened. It asks a different question: can the architecture that survived Investigate become a physical translation medium for an environment a person cannot feel directly?

**Not an electronic pet.**  
The first product instinct was companionship — Tamagotchi territory. That path was rejected in Investigate (`AL-I19`) and is rejected again here. The object does not exist to be loved. It exists to make an ecosystem’s state legible. If companionship appears, it is a by-product of that mechanism, not the brief (`RE-I1`, `RE-I5`).

**Not a claim that the network “learned ecology.”**  
Within this window, stress responses ride on designed sensory-weighting rules. Those rules are not the same thing as behaviour earned through long experience. The architecture keeps them separable on purpose (`RE-Q6`, `RE-I3`, `RE-I22`). Misrepresenting design as emergence would be the easiest way to make the project look stronger than it is — so this card forbids it.

**TYPE** 🟨 INSIGHT

## 4 · RE-Q1

**Title:** What must stay legible without a dashboard?

**Body:**

This is the Realise form of the problem Investigate already named (`AL-Q7`, and the earlier placeholder `RE-01` inside the field cards).

If the only way to understand the object is to open an app and read numbers, the project has failed its own brief. The field interviews were never really asking whether people would *like* a digital creature. They asked how a person first notices that a living system’s state has changed — seen, heard, smelled, touched, or “just felt” — and what it costs to learn that reading (`AL-P0`–`P4`).

So the constraint for Realise is concrete:

- The primary output is physical and non-verbal (light, breath, optionally extreme tone — never speech).
- A phone journal or PWA may exist as a secondary record for the portfolio and for debugging.
- Evaluation criterion: can a person standing next to it form a usable intuition about *what the environment is going through*, without being handed a chart?

Everything in the expression layer has to answer this question first. Charm is irrelevant. Clarity under stress is not.

## 5 · RE-Q2

**Title:** If the entity inhabits an ecosystem, whose feelings are being shown?

**Body:**

There are two easy mistakes, and both produce a product that looks like Unit 2 again.

**Mistake 1 — show the user’s feelings.**  
A device that brightens when you approach, soothes when you are sad, performs caretaking. That is companionship design. It centres the human emotional need.

**Mistake 2 — invent a pet’s feelings and ask the user to love them.**  
Same territory, different costume.

Realise chooses a third centre: **the environment’s stress, read through a species-shaped sensory weighting.**  
When the light turns urgent, it should mean *this soil / water / air is departing its own recent rhythm* — not *the object wants you*. The entity is a translation medium. It “inhabits” a genome the way an actor inhabits a role: the performance is about the world being sensed, not about winning attachment.

This sounds semantic until it hits the LED map. Every mapping decision after this card (`RE-I5`, `RE-I13`) is judged by one test: does the output report the environment, or audition for affection?

## 6 · RE-Q3

**Title:** Can one body wear many natures?

**Body:**

Investigate’s genome was temperament — eight parameters that biased how an individual entity felt. Realise keeps the word and changes the job.

Here a genome is **not different hardware**. It is a sensory weighting table: for each of the seven channels, a weight (0–1), a comfort range, and a sensitivity curve. Switch the JSON, and the same egg, the same probes, the same CfC core inhabit a different niche — soil/root, freshwater, indoor air.

Weight zero does not mean the sensor is off. The channel is still recorded. Zero only means *this nature does not care about that dimension* (`RE-I10`, `RE-I11`). That distinction matters later: a muted channel can still teach the system that the medium has changed, or that the wrong genome is loaded.

The “chameleon” claim stands or falls on this card. If I need a different board for water than for soil, I do not have one body wearing many natures — I have a product line.

**ID** `RE-Q3`

## 7 · RE-Q4

**Title:** Where do the thresholds come from — literature, or lived observation?

**Body:**

Hard-coded comfort ranges copied from a paper are convenient and weakly defensible. They also pretend the device arrived knowing what “normal” is for *this* patch of soil, *this* tank, *this* room.

Realise’s preferred answer: **auto-calibrate from observation during an initial deployment window.** The entity first becomes familiar with its environment’s own recent rhythm, then flags departures from that rhythm. That is closer to interoceptive / predictive logic already used in Investigate, and it maps cleanly onto a developmental metaphor without pretending the metaphor is biology.

This does not make thresholds “objective.” It makes their origin **local and inspectable**. Anyone reading the work can ask: calibrated on what days, under what interventions, with which genome loaded?

Literature still informs the *shape* of the curves and which channel combinations count as signature stress (drought, hypoxia risk, poor ventilation). Lived observation supplies the *centre* of the comfort band. Confusing those two sources is how a demo becomes a fiction.

**ID** `RE-Q4`  
**TYPE** 🟦 QUESTION

## 8 · RE-Q5

**Title:** What can five weeks honestly demonstrate?

**Body:**

A slow ecological trend — soil acidifying over months — cannot be “shown” inside a Realise window without lying with time. So the credible demo class is narrow on purpose:

**In scope:** short-timescale, induced events. Withhold water until moisture and coupled channels move. Shift temperature abruptly. Switch genome mid-session and show the same hardware re-weighting the same world.

**Out of scope for proof:** week-scale chemical drift, “the network learned a new ecological concept,” claims about whole-environment health from one probe tip.

This card exists so later demo cards (`RE-P1`–`P3`) cannot quietly inflate. The boundary is also written as insight in `RE-I3`. Q5 is the question form; I3 is the sworn statement.

Five weeks can still be enough — if what is demonstrated is *translation under induced stress*, not *mastery of ecology*.

## 9 · RE-Q6

**Title:** When light breathes, is that emergence or design?

**Body:**

Viewers will see breathing light and say “it feels alive.” That sentence is dangerous if I let it mean “the liquid network invented this behaviour.”

Split the stack:

1. **Designed layer** — sensory weighting rules, genome tables, LED mapping (hue ← valence, breath rate ← arousal, tremor ← safety, and so on). Authored. Tunable. Not earned by experience inside the five-week window.
2. **Dynamic layer** — CfC continuous-time response to the weighted stream: how the internal state moves when inputs change together, how it recovers, how it lags. This is where temporal dynamics can still be interesting without being oversold as “understanding drought.”

Realise’s honesty rule: **never present (1) as if it were (2).**  
Architecture must keep them separable in code and in language (`RE-I22`). If a drought demo looks right because I wrote a moisture weight of 0.9 and a red hue for negative valence, that is successful design — and I should call it design.

Emergence, if any, is a narrower claim about dynamics under those rules — not a synonym for “the lights look emotional.”

**DATE** 2026.7.1  
**ID** `RE-Q6`  
**TYPE** 🟦 QUESTION

## 10 · RE-I5

**Title:** Companionship Is a By-product, Not a Goal

**Body:**

Once `RE-Q2` is answered, the expression layer needs a hard line in plain language.

**Goal:** translate environmental state into a form a person can read with the body — glance, peripheral vision, gut response — without a dashboard (`RE-Q1`).

**Not a goal:** make the user fond of the object; mimic pet bonding loops; reward proximity with “happiness”; speak; perform neediness.

Companionship may still happen. People anthropomorphise glowing things. Field participants already reached for words like care, responsibility, and damage when they heard the speculative probe. That reaction is data about *human reading*, not permission to redesign the brief around attachment.

Design test I will reuse on every LED decision:  
If I remove the user’s desire to be liked from the story, does the behaviour still make sense as a report about soil, water, or air?  
If no — cut it.

This card is the operational twin of `RE-I1` (the pivot) and `AL-I19` (the rejection). I1 explains why the brief changed. This card explains how that change polices the build.
